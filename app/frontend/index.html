AI-Powered Dynamic Mental Health
Monitoring for Atrocity Victims

Victims of atrocities experience prolonged psychological distress long after complaint registration — due to threats, repeated court appearances, investigation delays, social ostracism and economic hardship. Existing support focuses only on legal and financial aid, with no continuous monitoring of victim well-being. SurakshaMind is a consent-based, multilingual AI system that conducts periodic check-ins, builds a personalized distress baseline, and predicts escalation before crisis — triggering explainable alerts to counsellors and district authorities in real time.

  • MFCC2 and Loudness are validated cross-linguistic PTSD markers (Kathan et al., Interspeech 2024) — elevated MFCC2 and reduced loudness correlate with hypoarousal states associated with chronic stress and PTSD.
  • Librosa extracts acoustic features from IVRS call recordings — MFCC coefficients, spectral centroid, RMS energy, and pause detection — without storing the raw audio.
  • PyTorch classifier maps the feature vector to a distress signal which feeds the same DDS formula as text check-in, enabling a unified score across channels.
